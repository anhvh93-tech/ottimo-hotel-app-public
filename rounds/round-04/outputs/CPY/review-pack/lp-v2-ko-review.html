For the reader

Thank you for reading this for us.

OTTIMO Boutique Stay is a calm place to stay on a quiet hillside in Da Lat, Viet Nam, for a few nights or a few months. Our voice is short, polite and calm: no exclamation marks, no emoji, no hard selling. We are not a hotel chain and we do not rent out flats; please flag any word that sounds like either.

  1. Read each line in Korean as a guest would, and compare it with the English.
  2. If a line is wrong, unnatural or sounds machine-translated, write your version or a comment in the Comments box. Leave the box empty when the line is fine.
  3. Lines marked Legal: please correct the language only, not the meaning. Our lawyer checks the meaning.
  4. Lines marked Screen reader are not visible; software reads them aloud to blind visitors. They should still sound natural.
  5. Keep {name}, {amount}, {date}, {time} and {holder} as they are: the page fills them in. Times, percentages and numbers of days are fixed facts; tell us only if the format looks unusual.
  6. Lines marked Plural check repeat with different numbers to test the word endings.

Sending your comments back. Type in the boxes; your notes stay in this browser on this device. When you finish, press Download comments and send the file to the person who gave you this page, or press Copy comments and paste them into a message. On paper: print, write beside the lines, and send a photo. Or simply message us the line numbers with your corrections.
